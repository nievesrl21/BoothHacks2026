Who judges each prize

Tick the judges for each category prize and pick a lead. Those judges rate Prize Fit, and only their scores count toward that prize. Best Overall always uses every judge. Changes save instantly.

Edit prizes

Prizes appear as checkboxes on the student site and as filters for judges. The Prize Fit text is what judges see on the 3 / 2 / 1 buttons. With a Prize Fit weight of 30 and the default criteria, Prize Fit is 30% of a category score.

Judges rate each criterion 3 Strong, 2 Adequate or 1 Weak. Best Overall uses the first weight; category prizes use the second weight plus Prize Fit. Weights don't need to add up to 100: every total is scaled to 300. Totals recalculate if you change them.

To add a judge: 1. In Supabase, open Authentication → Users → Add user, and create their login with their email and a password. 2. Add them here with the same email. 3. In the Prizes tab, tick them on the prizes they judge. Then send them this site's link with their email and password.
